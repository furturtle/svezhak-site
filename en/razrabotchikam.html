Our rules

  • Every paid placement is labeled “Ad” (and carries an erid marker as Russian law requires).
  • We never change Steam reviews, scores or rankings: they stay honest.
  • Games are only bought on Steam; we sell nothing.
  • We don't feature adult or explicit games.

How to get featured

  1. Message us: game title, Steam link and the format you're interested in.
  2. We agree on dates, copy and visuals.
  3. We publish! New players discover your game, and you watch wishlists and sales roll in on Steamworks 🎉
